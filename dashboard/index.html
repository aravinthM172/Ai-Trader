Switch MT5 account

Logs MetaTrader on the VPS in to another account. The bot's history for the current account is archived, and trading stays paused until you press Start. The password goes to the VPS once and is not stored.
Lot sizes follow the account's balance by themselves. The size sets the base for the daily and total loss limits; the switch is undone if MT5's balance is not within 90–115 % of it.
Account type
Real money: the bot will place real orders on this account after you press Start. Most prop-firm accounts (FundingPips included) are Demo type even when funded; MT5 shows the type, and the switch is undone if it does not match.
