Switch MT5 account

Logs MetaTrader on the VPS in to another account. The bot's history for the current account is archived, and trading stays paused until you press Start. The password goes to the VPS once and is not stored.
Lot sizes follow the account's balance by themselves. The size sets the base for the daily and total loss limits; the switch is undone if MT5's balance is not within 90–115 % of it.
Account type
Real money: the bot will place real orders on this account after you press Start. Prop-firm accounts differ (FundedNext shows as Real, others as Demo even when funded); MT5 shows the type, and the switch is undone if it does not match.

Live vs backtest

Backtest = same strategy on years of broker history with real spread and swap. Only symbols marked "approved" may trade.
Live calculator: uses each market's ATR (last closed hour), its contract size and your live equity — the same maths the bot uses. Changes apply to new trades only; open trades keep their stop and target. No trade can ever risk more than 1 % of equity — a fixed lot that would is cut down.

Auto risk

Risk shrinks as the account nears its loss limit (down to 0.3×) and grows with profit (up to 1.5×). Backtest: 0 % challenge fails vs 2 % at fixed risk.
